`fitment`: `VehicleCombobox`, an accessible combobox for the vehicle selector (make/model/year). Shows logos in options and in the field, puts a popular-makes grid on top, supports search with type-ahead and full keyboard control, and opens as a bottom sheet on mobile. Progressive enhancement: renders a native `<select>` on the server and without JavaScript.
